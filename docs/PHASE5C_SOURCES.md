## Scope and corrections

- **Locations:** rechecked Named Locations and Landmarks access and retained the prior Chapter 1–7 tables, OG/Remix distinctions, Chapter 7 snapshots and Epic landmark correction. No season-level row is converted to launch presence. Event-location edges extend world history without asserting new introductions, destruction or identity continuity.
- **LTMs:** reviewed all 82 non-music/non-Creative index tiles and their available linked profiles. Demon Rush redirects to Horde Rush; Solid Gold V2 does not create another canonical answer. Fog of War/Sneaky Silencers and the index's combined Bounty/Wick/Mando labels retain aliases. Team Rumble and Trios have explicit historical LTM origins but remain labelled permanent former LTMs. Late Game Arena is explicitly in the index's former-LTM population and separately retains its rotating ranked classification; Bugha's Late Game is explicitly described as an LTM. This does not classify every ranked playlist as an LTM. Fall Guys Crown Jam and Creative's The Combine are excluded from the BR scope. Concert/Creative experiences, tournaments, event playlists and core Battle Royale/Zero Build are not imported as LTMs.
- **LTM rules:** 10 Large-Team subgroup memberships and positive respawn-profile rules become reusable mechanic edges. The subgroup is **not** the complete answer population for “teams larger than a squad”: special modes such as Food Fight, Endgame and Day of Doom also qualify. That question is pending. Missing profiles/dates and incomplete rule/return histories do not acquire guessed chronology. Sixty-one profile release labels are retained as labels, including patch-linked labels; these are not all exact calendar dates.
- **Events:** 67 main BR live events/story moments are separate from Party Royale and Creative concert tables. Major events, chapter finales and concerts also support the existing live-event classification; smaller story moments do not automatically become major events. Same-named events in different chapters retain distinct identities. The announced December 5, 2026 THE TREASURE event is excluded from happened-in-the-past facts. C6 Galactic Battle and Simpsons event scopes use their special-season IDs. Mere event presence supplies `eventAt`; only four explicit alteration clauses supply `eventTransforms`, with partial-building conditions. An abduction does not prove total POI destruction.
- **Wildlife:** the index table alone is incomplete: its full article explicitly describes Crows as interactive wildlife. Added that positive identity, collectable firefly swarms from the Firefly Jar profile, and Supply Llamas' documented former Animal role. The final 19-type roster excludes ambient-only animals and cosmetic pets/contrails. Fireflies' cosmetic page was not used as animal evidence. Air/Water/Earth Sprite names are accepted under the canonical Sprites umbrella, without counting three extra answers. Controlled riding, taming and hostility stay separate; a fishing-rod-controlled shark is not automatically tameable. Supply Llama remains its original container ID with an additional historical creature role.
- **Equipment:** compared all 449 utility/consumable/trap rows, including the mode-excluded tails. The five apparent identity misses resolved to four Icecream/Ice Cream spelling aliases and one genuinely absent Explosive Batarang. Its original profile explicitly establishes Season X, despite the index showing its OG return. It remains an uncategorized gameplay item, not a fabricated gun class. Thirteen BR trap identities are reviewed; Creative-only rails, Speed Boost, Ice Block and Super Launch Pad are excluded. Source errors such as the OG campfire's copied launch description are not promoted as effects. This is not a full season-by-season weapon loot-pool review.
- **Overdrive Grenade:** identity already existed in Phase 5B. Its individual profile now verifies throwable, knockback, launch, rotation, speed and **initial-launch-only** fall protection, C7S2 introduction and appearance. Overdrive status does not grant general fall immunity. No reload-speed or fire-rate claim is promoted. Epic's [Showdown announcement](https://www.fortnite.com/news/showdown-in-the-new-fortnite-battle-royale-season) independently names it as a movement item. Conditional proof is retained both on the field and graph edge.
- **Businesses:** rechecked all 90 company category members and profiles. Fifty-three actual restaurants, shops, dealerships, markets, food trucks or gas businesses qualify; producer-only brands, insurers, hotels, STW-only Ballers Pub and the advertisement-only One Shot Sports are not assumed to be stores. Articles identify Fork Knife/Joel's Pub/Snow Cones/Puppuccino retail roles even when their infobox type is missing or vague. Branch edges join only existing reviewed BR places, excluding Stonewood and lore realities. They remain undated; infobox location lists do not establish continuous operation or original introduction. A chain/company entity is separate from a building or parent POI.
- **Containers:** all original 75 identities remain. Fortune Sprite Chest and Cursed Sprite Chest add two documented special-island types. Standard/Rare treasure-island textures, five Mothership rarity labels, and Exotic Holo-Chest remain canonical aliases, not more answers. Faction Chest is an umbrella, not another type. Environmental stars, eggs, targets, spawners, posters and cannons are retained separately as positive loot-source facts. Extraction Chest drops no loot and is excluded. Named loot links create positive `providesLoot` edges; “high-tier loot” does not generate guessed inventories. Introduction-chapter scopes use the earliest explicit index row for the canonical identity; later texture/variant rows do not reintroduce the object originally.

## Repeatable review gate

The research script writes **candidates under `/tmp` by default**, never the live reviewed snapshot. The diff script compares candidate identities with canonical names/aliases and writes a report, without importing entries into the game. On the delivered reviewed snapshot, 344 raw rows already match, 105 are mode-excluded candidates and zero unresolved identity candidates remain. Raw rows are not independent entities. Classification, introductions, mode membership and full populations still require human review.

```sh
python scripts/research-phase5c.py
CATALOG_RESEARCH_INPUT=/tmp/p5c/research-candidates.json \
  CATALOG_DIFF_OUTPUT=/tmp/phase5c-next-catalog-diff.json npm run audit:catalog-diff
```

Review revisions, profile contradictions, canonical aliases, dates, gameplay conditions and complete populations before updating the committed snapshot or admitting a field. Set `PHASE5C_CACHE_DIR=/tmp/p5c-next-review` to use a fresh cache when checking for new source revisions; `/tmp/p5c` deliberately caches responses for the current review. The scripts perform no runtime fetches in the game.
